September 2026 Update
In my heart, I will forever keep dancing toward perfection.

PixelOS Seventeen continues to move forward. With the initial Android 17 release behind us, the September update is where things start settling into place, with further polish, fixes, and improvements across the system.

This release should also mark the start of a broader Android 17 rollout across our device roster. More supported devices are expected to receive their first PixelOS Seventeen builds over the coming weeks, as maintainers complete their Android 17 bring-up.

Stay tuned!

What's new?

September security patch

We've merged the September Android Security Bulletin into PixelOS, updating the platform security level to 2026-09-01. This covers the usual spread of security fixes across the framework, media, connectivity, and system components.

Mobile data "Don't ask again"

The mobile data QS tile now shows a Pixel-style confirmation dialog when toggling mobile data on, with a "Don't ask again" option that persists your choice per user.

New Translations

PixelOS is now available in even more languages. Thanks to contributions from our community on Crowdin, we've added and improved translations for Ukrainian, Polish, Slovak, Korean, several regional Chinese variants, and more. A huge thank you to all the volunteers for dedicating their time and effort to making PixelOS accessible to users around the world. We hope to keep expanding language support with every release. You can contribute translations on Crowdin for the languages that you know.
